Refund policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026. If Remembra isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment and you get it back.

14 days on your first payment

Every paid plan (Solo, Pro, Team and the Founding 100 offer, monthly or yearly) comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee on its first payment. Ask within 14 days of that payment and we refund it in full. You don't need to give a reason.

A refund ends the plan at once: the account goes back to Free, and any credits left from the paid plan, including a yearly credit bank, go with it. Your notes, handoffs and trail stay in the account, within the Free plan's limits.

Renewals and cancelling

  • After the first 14 days, payments are not refundable, including renewals, and we don't refund part of a month or year.
  • You can cancel at any time from Billing in the dashboard. The plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then the account moves to Free. Nothing renews after you cancel.
  • If something on our side stopped you using what you paid for, write to us. We look at those cases one by one.

How to ask

Email support@remembra.dev from the address on your Remembra account, and include the order number from your Paddle receipt if you have it. You can also ask Paddle directly at paddle.net.

Paddle sends the money back to the card or account you paid with. It usually shows up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

We may turn down requests that are part of a pattern of abuse, such as buying and refunding the same plan again and again.

Paddle and your statutory rights

Our order process is run by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record, and every refund is paid by Paddle. Paddle's buyer terms and refund policy also apply to your purchase.

If you are a consumer in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, the law may give you a right to withdraw from the purchase; Paddle's buyer terms explain how it works for digital services. This policy adds to those rights and never takes any away.

Chargebacks

Please ask us before you dispute a charge with your bank; a refund is usually faster. A chargeback ends the paid plan the same way a refund does.
