Credits, in plain words

You only pay for what's new.

Always free, on every plan

  • Handoffs, when an agent stops
  • Pickups, when the next agent starts
  • The inbox between your agents
  • The trail of every session
  • Searching your memory

Uses smart credits

  • AI enrichment of a new note: pulling out the facts, people and projects in it so search can connect them later

One credit is about one normal note. A note up to 8,000 characters (a few pages) uses one credit, and 8,000 characters is the longest note Free takes. On paid plans, where notes run to 50,000 characters, a longer note uses one credit for each 8,000 characters. A fact-dense note on any plan can use more: the note's actual AI cost, when that is larger.

Run out, and nothing breaks. Your notes still save and stay searchable, and your handoffs keep working; new notes just skip the AI enrichment until your credits renew. Nothing is rejected for credits, and your dashboard shows where you stand.

Self-hosting is free. The core is open source under the MIT license. Run it on your own server with your own AI key and there is nothing to meter.

Questions

Short answers.

Do handoffs ever cost credits?
No. Handoffs, checkpoints, pickups, the inbox, the trail and search never use credits on any plan. Each plan has a fair-use soft cap on handoff events a month; going over it is reported, not rejected. A per-minute burst limit (30 a minute on Free, 60 on Solo, 120 on Pro and Team) slows a runaway script, and on Free handoffs count toward the 300 unenriched saves a day.
What happens when my credits run out?
Your notes keep saving without AI enrichment until your credits renew, and they stay searchable. Running out of credits never blocks a save. A save is refused only at your plan's limits: the notes-kept cap, the note length (8,000 characters on Free, 50,000 on paid plans), the project count (3 on Free), the notes per request (10 on Free, 100 on paid plans), and on Free, 300 unenriched saves a day.
What changes on a yearly plan?
You pay for 10 months instead of 12, and you get the whole year's credits on day one to use in any month. A refund or chargeback ends the plan and its credit bank at once.
Is a card required for Free?
No. Free is $0 with no card. New Free accounts may start with 25 credits until the email address is verified.
Do prices include tax?
No. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.
Can I run it myself?
Yes. The core is open source (MIT). Self-hosting guide.
