Credits, in plain words

You only pay for what's new.

Always free, on every plan

  • Handoffs, when an agent stops
  • Pickups, when the next agent starts
  • The inbox between your agents
  • The trail of every session
  • Searching your memory

Uses credits

  • AI enrichment of a new note: pulling out the facts, people and projects in it so search can connect them later

One credit is about one normal note. A note up to 8,000 characters (a few pages) uses one credit. Longer notes use one credit for each 8,000 characters.

Run out, and nothing breaks. Your notes still save and your handoffs keep working; new notes just skip the AI enrichment until your credits refresh. Nothing is ever rejected for credits, and your dashboard shows where you stand.

Self-hosting is free. The core is open source under the MIT license. Run it on your own server with your own AI key and there is nothing to meter.

Questions

Short answers.

Do handoffs ever cost credits?
No. Handoffs, pickups, the inbox, the trail and search never use credits on any plan. Each plan has a generous fair-use limit on how many handoff events it sends a month.
What happens when my credits run out?
Your notes keep saving without AI enrichment until your credits refresh, or until you add a Boost pack on a paid plan. Nothing is blocked.
Is there a card required for Free?
No. Free is $0 with no card.
Can I run it myself?
Yes. The core is open source (MIT). Self-hosting guide.
