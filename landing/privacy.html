The short version

  • Self-hosted? Your server sends us nothing.
  • On Remembra Cloud, we keep your account, your notes, your handoffs and what we need to bill you.
  • Text you store is sent to OpenAI to make it searchable and, for notes, to pull out facts and names. OpenAI does not train on it under its API terms.
  • We name everyone else who handles your data on the subprocessors page.
  • We don't sell your data, show you ads, or run analytics scripts on our websites.
  • Your data is stored in the United States.

Who we are

Remembra is made by DolphyTech ("we", "us"), 515 Centerpoint Drive, Suite #212, Middletown, CT 06457, USA. This policy covers our website (remembra.dev), our docs (docs.remembra.dev), the dashboard (app.remembra.dev) and the hosted service, Remembra Cloud (api.remembra.dev).

We decide how your account and billing information is used, so for that we are the controller. If you use Remembra Cloud to store information about other people for your organization, we process that content on your instructions; our data processing agreement covers that.

What we collect

When you visit our websites

  • Request logs. Our web server and Cloudflare, which sits in front of it, record each request: IP address, time, page, referrer and browser. We use them to keep the sites running and to stop abuse.
  • Fonts. remembra.dev loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads. We plan to host the fonts ourselves. The docs site uses your system fonts and sends nothing to Google.
  • No analytics or ad trackers. Neither site runs analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

When you write to us

  • The contact form sends your name, email, company (if given) and message through formsubmit.co to contact@remembra.dev.
  • Email to our remembra.dev addresses (support@, sales@, contact@) passes through Namecheap's email forwarding. Our dolphytech.com addresses (admin@, security@) are hosted by Google Workspace. Both are on the subprocessors page.
  • Email you send us is kept in our inbox.

When you use Remembra Cloud

  • Account: your email address, an optional name, a hash of your password (never the password), and, if you sign in with GitHub or Google, your account id and verified email from that provider. If you turn on two-factor sign-in, its secret is stored encrypted.
  • API keys: the keys you create, with the agent and projects each is limited to.
  • What you store: notes and memories, the facts and names pulled out of them, handoffs and checkpoints your agents leave, inbox messages between agents, and the project and repository names they belong to.
  • Handoffs: when an agent's session ends, remembra-relay reads facts from git on your machine (branch, commit ids and messages, changed file names, whether commits are pushed) and, for Claude Code, from the local session transcript (commands run, test results, open items). Secrets and personal data the redaction rules catch are removed before anything is sent. The transcript itself is never uploaded.
  • Usage: API requests, credits used, storage used and errors, for billing, plan limits and fixing problems. Our security log records sign-ins, key changes and other account events with the IP address that made them.
  • Pickup events: when an agent's session brief shows a handoff another agent left, we record which agents, which handoff and when (ids and times, never the handoff's content). They show you who picked up each handoff, and tell us, in aggregate, whether handoffs are being picked up. They are deleted with the handoff.
  • Billing: Paddle takes the payment. It tells us your email, plan, country, subscription status and whether a payment went through. Your card details go to Paddle, never to us.

What goes to OpenAI

Remembra Cloud uses OpenAI's API for two things:

  • Search. The text of each note and handoff you store is sent to OpenAI to compute an embedding, the list of numbers that makes it searchable. Your search queries are embedded the same way.
  • Enrichment. While you have credits, the text of each new note is sent to OpenAI's language models to pull out facts, people and projects and to merge duplicates. Handoffs are not enriched.

OpenAI processes this data for us under its API terms. It says it does not use API data to train its models unless the customer opts in (we have not), and that it may keep API data for up to 30 days to detect abuse. See how OpenAI handles API data.

Why we use it

We use your information only to:

  • Run the service you signed up for: store and search your memory, build briefs and handoffs, bill your plan. (For visitors in the EEA and UK, the legal basis is our contract with you.)
  • Keep it secure: detect and stop abuse, fraud and attacks, and keep security logs. (Our legitimate interest in a secure service.)
  • Fix and improve it: look at errors and aggregate usage, not the content of your notes. (Our legitimate interest.)
  • Tell you what you need to know: email verification, password resets, security notices and billing notices. We do not send marketing email without your consent.
  • Meet legal obligations, such as tax records, which Paddle keeps as the seller. (Legal obligation.)

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use what you store to train AI models. We look at the content of your account only when you ask us to for support, or when the law requires it.

Who else handles it

A small number of companies process data for us: hosting, the network edge, AI processing, email and payments. Each is named, with what it receives and where, on the subprocessors page. We will update that page before adding a new one.

Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) sells our paid plans as Merchant of Record. It receives your billing details (name, email, address, payment method) to take payment, charge tax and handle refunds, and it handles them under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people from harm, or to a buyer if the business is sold, in which case we will tell you first.

Where it is stored

Remembra Cloud runs on servers rented from Hetzner in the United States. If you use it from outside the US, your data is transferred to and stored in the US. For transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, which our DPA includes. We do not offer an EU data region yet.

How it is protected

  • In transit: every connection to our sites and API uses HTTPS.
  • At rest: memory content and metadata in our vector store are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and two-factor secrets are encrypted. The database that holds the other copy of your notes and your account records is not yet field-encrypted; we are extending encryption to it.
  • Redaction: keys, tokens and other secrets are stripped from what you store before it is saved.
  • Access: each account's data is kept apart, and API keys can be limited to one agent and to certain projects.

Remembra has not had a SOC 2 audit or an outside penetration test. The security page says what we do today, what we don't do yet, and how to report a problem. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay and as the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you store are kept while your account is open. Handoffs and structured checkpoints do not expire: they stay on your trail until you delete them or your account is erased. Free-text checkpoints expire after 7 days by default.
  • Deleting your account: deleting it from the dashboard today deactivates it and revokes its keys, but does not yet erase what is stored. To have everything erased, email admin@dolphytech.com from the account's address and we will erase it within 30 days. Backup copies are removed as backups are replaced.
  • Request logs and security logs are kept only as long as we need them to run and protect the service.
  • Billing records are kept by Paddle for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to how we use it, or hand it over in a machine-readable format. You can export your memories yourself through the API. For anything else, email admin@dolphytech.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK and think we got it wrong, you can complain to your data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines those terms.

Cookies and local storage

Our websites set no cookies of their own. Cloudflare, which protects them, may set a cookie its bot checks need. Your browser's local storage keeps your light or dark theme choice, and the pricing page remembers the monthly or yearly toggle for the visit. Nothing in them identifies you, and they never leave your browser.

The dashboard keeps your sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Signing in with GitHub or Google uses short-lived cookies that tie the sign-in to your browser; they are cleared when sign-in finishes. None of these are used for tracking or advertising.

Self-hosted Remembra

If you run Remembra on your own servers, it sends us nothing: your memories, keys and logs stay with you. It sends text to the AI provider you configure, under your own account with them.

Children

Remembra is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will email account holders before it takes effect.
